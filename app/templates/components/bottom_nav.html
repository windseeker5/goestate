{# Mobile bottom tab bar (thumb-zone navigation). Replaces the top hamburger as the primary way to move around the app on a phone. Nav used to be two taps to anywhere, behind a drawer anchored at the top of a tall screen — the hardest spot to reach one-handed. This puts the daily destinations one tap away in the bottom third of the screen, the way native apps do it. Hidden at md+ (768px), matching Basecoat's hardcoded sidebar breakpoint — above that the sidebar is pinned open and this bar would be redundant. Keep that breakpoint in sync with layouts/app.html. "More" toggles the existing sidebar drawer, so every destination that isn't a tab (Assets, Liabilities, Documents, Settings) stays reachable — no nav target is lost. Usage: {% from "components/bottom_nav.html" import bottom_nav %} {{ bottom_nav(items=tab_items) }} Params: items {array} Up to 4 item dicts from the `nav_items` list in layouts/app.html — each with label / url / icon / current. Icons and `current` predicates are reused from there rather than duplicated here, so the two navs can't drift apart. #} {# z-30, NOT z-40. Basecoat layers the open drawer as scrim 40 -> panel 50, but the scrim's z-index makes .sidebar a stacking context, which traps the panel's 50 inside it. A z-40 bar here therefore ties with the scrim and — coming later in the DOM — paints over the whole drawer, silently burying the Appearance control and Sign out in its footer. Raising the panel cannot fix that; the bar has to sit below the scrim. Nothing in the app occupies 30-40. Layer order overall: bar 30 < scrim 40 < panel/popovers 50 < toasts 60. #} {% macro bottom_nav(items) %}
