# Money — Cheat Sheet

The **Money** page (sidebar) answers three questions:

- **How much is in the estate bank account?**
- **How much does each person still hold for the estate?** (sale money not yet deposited)
- **Where did the money come from and where did it go?** (totals by category, for the heirs)

Golden rule: **enter things the way the bank shows them**, on the day they happened.

---

## Every transaction is ONE step

Ask yourself one question: **"What did the bank do?"** Then do the one thing in the table. Nothing else.

| What happened at the bank | The ONE step |
|---|---|
| **Money came in** (cheque, insurance, refund, interest) | **Money → Add entry** → type the amount and date → **Save**. If the app asks **"Is this money you were expecting?"**, click **Yes** |
| **I deposited cash I was holding** (sale money) | **Money → Record deposit** |
| **A bill was paid** (Hydro, notary…) | **Liabilities** → the bill → **Payments → Record payment** |
| **The bank charged a fee** | **Money → Add entry** → category **Bank fees** |
| **Money I got from selling an item, kept in my pocket** | **Assets** → the item → Status **Sold**, Sale Price, **Assigned to** me |

### Two things NOT to do

- **Never change an item's Status to "In bank account" by hand.** The app no longer lets you. Changing a word doesn't put money in the bank. The app sets that status for you when you answer **Yes** above.
- **Never enter the same money twice** (once on the item and once in Money). If the item is still waiting, use the **Yes** answer and the app links them for you.

> **Alternative door, same result:** open the item in **Assets** and press **Money received**. The amount is pre-filled. Press **Save**. This is also one step.

## First time only: the starting balance

1. **Money** → *Estate bank account* card.
2. **Balance on the printout:** the balance on your latest bank printout or receipt.
3. **Date of the printout:** its date.
4. **Statement PDF:** upload the scan, or pick it under *…or one already in Documents*.
5. **Save starting balance.**

Forgot the PDF? The card shows **Statement: None attached** with an **Attach statement** button.

> Anything dated **on or before** that date is treated as already inside that
> balance. Use a printout taken **after** your operations that day. A deposit
> receipt usually shows the new balance.

## Example: my trip to the bank yesterday

I deposited 2 cheques for items already sold, paid Hydro-Québec, and deposited
$1,000 cash I was holding.

| What I did at the bank | Where in the app | Fill in |
|---|---|---|
| Deposited **cheque 1** (sold item) | **Money → Record deposit** | Who: **me** · Amount: cheque amount · Date: yesterday · Note: "Cheque — *item name*" |
| Deposited **cheque 2** (sold item) | **Money → Record deposit** | Same as above |
| Deposited **$1,000 cash** | **Money → Record deposit** | Who: **me** · Amount: **1000** · Date: yesterday · Note: "Cash" |
| Paid **Hydro-Québec** | **Liabilities →** the Hydro bill **→ Payments** | Amount · Date paid: yesterday · Paid with: **Estate bank account** · Category: **House, car & utilities** → **Record payment** |
| The bank charged **$5** for it | **Money → Add entry** | Category: **Bank fees** · Amount: **5** · Date: yesterday (see *Bank fees* below) |

**Before the deposits:** the items must be marked **Sold**, with a **Sale Price** and **Assigned to = me**. Otherwise the money was never counted as "held by me".

**If Hydro isn't in Liabilities yet:** **Liabilities → Add liability** first, then record the payment on it.

**Result:** what I hold drops by the 2 cheques + $1,000, and Hydro shows **Paid**.

> **Tip:** if the bank grouped several items into **one** deposit (one line on
> the statement), record **one** deposit with the total. Matching the
> statement line by line makes the bank check easy.

## Everyday situations

| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| I sold an item and kept the money | **Assets** → item → Status **Sold**, Sale Price, **Assigned to** = who got the money |
| I put held money in the bank | **Money → Record deposit** |
| A bill was paid from the estate account | **Liabilities** → bill → **Record payment**, Paid with **Estate bank account** |
| I paid a bill with sale money I held | Same, but Paid with **Cash held by *me*** (what I owe goes down) |
| A cheque or benefit you were waiting for reached the bank (death benefit, insurance refund) | **Money → Add entry** (Account: Estate bank account, a *Money in* category, the amount, the date). The app asks **"Is this money you were expecting?"**: say **Yes** and the item is marked *In bank account* for you. Or open the item in **Assets** and press **Money received**. Don't change the item's Status yourself |
| Money arrived in the account (insurance, refund, tax) | **Money → Add entry** → pick its category under *Money in* (e.g. Insurance & pensions) |
| Bank fee or interest | **Money → Add entry** → Category **Bank fees** / **Interest** |
| Expense with no bill in Liabilities | **Money → Add entry** → pick its category under *Money out* (e.g. Other expenses) |
| I made a mistake | On the Money page, **Delete** the line and enter it again. For a sale, edit the asset instead |

## Bank fees and other charges

Banks add their own lines: a fee for a counter payment, a monthly account fee,
interest, sometimes a fee refunded. Each one goes in as **its own line**, the
way the statement shows it.

**Example: the bank charged $5 for paying Hydro-Québec at the counter.**

1. **Money → Add entry**.
2. Fill in:
    - **Account:** Estate bank account
    - **Category:** Bank fees. The category knows the money goes out.
    - **Amount:** 5
    - **Date:** the day the bank charged it
    - **Note:** "Fee — Hydro payment at the counter"
3. **Save**. The estate balance goes down by $5.

| The bank… | Category |
|---|---|
| charged a fee | **Bank fees** (money out) |
| paid interest | **Interest** (money in) |
| refunded a fee | **Correction — adds money** (note: "Fee refunded") |

> **Keep the fee separate from the bill.** Hydro stays at its amount, and the
> $5 is its own line, exactly like on the statement. Don't add them together.

**Forgot a fee? The bank check will catch it.** The **Difference** won't be
$0.00. It will be off by exactly that amount (e.g. **−$5.00**). Click **Add it**,
enter the fee, come back and tick it. You don't need to chase every fee as it
happens.

## Categories: where the money came from and went

Every sale, bill payment, fee and amount received gets **one category**. At
the end, the Money page gives the totals the notary, the accountant and the
heirs need. **Deposits don't get one**: they only move money from a person to
the bank.

| Money in | Money out |
|---|---|
| **Sale of items** (automatic, from Sold assets) | **Funeral & burial** |
| **Bank accounts & investments** | **House, car & utilities** (Hydro, internet, insurance, taxes, repairs) |
| **Insurance & pensions** (life insurance, RRQ) | **Legal & professional fees** (notary, accountant, RDPRM) |
| **Government refunds & benefits** | **Selling costs** (DuProprio, agent) |
| **Interest** | **Taxes owed** (final tax returns) |
| **Other money in** | **Debts of the deceased** (credit cards, loans) |
| | **Bank fees** |
| | **Other expenses** (stamps, mileage) |

**Where you pick it:**

- **A bill:** on the bill (Add or Edit liability) or when you **Record payment**. You pick it once and the payments follow. Change the bill's category later and its payments change too.
- **Add entry:** the **Category** list replaces "type" and "coming in / going out". The category decides the direction.
- **Not sure?** Click the **?** next to *Category* for examples of each one.

**Where you see it:**

- **Money → Money in and out:** totals for each category (the notary's *recettes et débours*).
- **Settings → Export → Money:** a *category* column for the accountant.

> **Correction — adds / removes money** is not a category. Use it only to fix
> a balance, e.g. a typo found during the bank check. It is left out of the
> totals.

## Checking against the bank (any time — weekly, monthly…)

1. **Money → Check with the bank.**
2. Type the **statement date** and the **balance printed on it** → **Next**.
3. **Tick** each line that appears on the statement.
4. Watch **Difference**:

    - **$0.00** → attach the statement (upload it, or pick it from Documents) → **Finish check**. Those lines become **Checked** and locked.
    - Not zero → a line is missing (e.g. a bank fee: click *Add it*, Bank fees is already selected) or a line isn't on the statement yet (untick it, it will wait for the next check).

5. Wrong check? **Money → Bank checks → Undo** (latest one only).

---

## Good to know

- **Estimated net estate** (top of the dashboard) = items still to sell + estate bank account + cash held − bills still to pay. Each dollar counts once: Sold and *In bank account* assets aren't counted as items, because their money is already in the bank or someone's hands.
- **Viewers** can see everything but can't change anything.
- The **dashboard** shows "You hold $X for the estate" with a **Record deposit** button.
- **Chat** knows the balances. Ask: *"How much does Ken still owe the estate?"*
- **Export:** **Settings → Export → Money** (CSV, for the final account to the heirs).
- **Before big changes:** **Settings → Download full backup (.zip)**.
