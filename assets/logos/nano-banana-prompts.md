# Estate Copilot — "EC" Logo Prompts for Google Nano Banana

Context baked into every prompt: a lettermark combining the letters **E** and **C**
(for "Estate Copilot"), rendered in believable 3D space, but with the restraint
of a professional geometric wordmark (think pi.dev, Vercel, Linear) — not a
cartoon mascot, not a gradient-soup SPA logo. No taglines, no extra text, no
drop-shadowed UI chrome. Just the mark, centered, on a plain background.

Paste any one of these directly into Nano Banana. Each targets a different
material/style "temperature" so you get real variation across the batch.

---

### 1. Baseline — Flat-to-3D Block Monogram
A minimalist 3D logo icon combining the letters "E" and "C" into a single interlocking monogram, built from thick geometric rectangular blocks on a strict grid, similar in spirit to the flat pixel-block wordmark used by pi.dev. Rendered with very slight 3D depth/extrusion, soft studio lighting, single flat color (deep charcoal or indigo), matte finish, no gradients, no bevels, centered on a plain white background. Ultra-clean, professional tech-brand quality, vector-flat aesthetic with subtle dimensionality.

### 2. Isometric Cube
A 3D isometric logo icon where the letters "E" and "C" are constructed from stacked isometric cubes, viewed from a classic 30-degree isometric angle, single solid color with only two tonal shades (light top face, darker side face) to imply depth — no photorealistic shading. Clean geometric precision, sharp edges, no clutter, centered on a plain neutral background, professional SaaS product branding style.

### 3. Extruded Typography, Soft Shadow
A modern 3D logo mark combining a bold geometric "E" and "C" as one continuous extruded typographic form, moderate extrusion depth, soft ambient occlusion shadow beneath, single muted color (slate gray or deep indigo), subtle matte plastic material, studio three-point lighting, centered on a clean light gray background. Precise, minimal, high-craft industrial design feel — like an Apple or Linear product icon.

### 4. Glass / Frosted Acrylic

Premium, quiet, minimal — no rainbow refractions, no excessive gloss.

### 5. Brutalist Concrete Block

Brutalist architecture aesthetic, serious and confident.

Elegant, tactile, precise geometric folds only — no curves.

### 9. Blueprint Wireframe Isometric

### 10. Interlocking Negative-Space Volumes
A minimalist 3D logo icon where solid geometric volumes form the letters "E" and "C" by interlocking through negative space — the gap between the two letters is as deliberate as the shapes themselves, similar in spirit to how pi.dev separates the "P" and "i" with a shifted block. ## Notes on getting good results from Nano Banana
- If a result is close but too busy, append: `remove all extra detail, simplify to the essential geometric form only`
- If lighting looks too dramatic/cinematic, append: `flat even studio lighting, no dramatic shadows`
- If it adds text/taglines you don't want, append: `no text, no letters other than E and C, no wordmark, icon only`
- To push "temperature"/originality further on any prompt, append: `unexpected but refined material choice, avoid generic gradient tech-logo cliches`
